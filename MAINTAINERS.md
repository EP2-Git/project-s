# Project S maintainers

Project S does not currently claim a larger maintainer team. Review requests can
be opened publicly, but response times are not guaranteed during pre-alpha.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainer responsibility is earned through sustained contribution rather than
a single large change. Evidence includes:

- correct and reviewable implementation;
- careful treatment of authority, privacy, RLS, and compatibility;
- useful review of other contributors' work;
- reliable issue triage and respectful community conduct; and
- willingness to own regressions, documentation, and release follow-through.

The lead maintainer may first delegate triage or an implementation area, then
broaden responsibility as trust and review quality accumulate. See
[governance](GOVERNANCE.md) for decision boundaries.

## Security

Do not request or disclose vulnerability details in a public maintainer thread.
Use the [private vulnerability reporting path](SECURITY.md).
