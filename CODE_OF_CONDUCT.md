# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal or security-sensitive information.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope, safety, and community boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, impersonation, or sustained bad-faith conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[private advisory form](https://github.com/EP2-Git/project-s/security/advisories/new)
and begin the title with `Conduct report`. This is a temporary private intake
route until a dedicated conduct mailbox exists; do not publish sensitive conduct
details in an issue or discussion. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions,
lock discussions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently exclude
participants. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
